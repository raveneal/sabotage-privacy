Sabotage — Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 September 2026

The short version. Sabotage does not ask for your name, your email or a password. It creates an anonymous account so your progress and your place on the leaderboard belong to you rather than to the phone. It contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting software of any kind. Advertising is opt-in only — you watch an ad because you chose to, and there are no interstitials. The microphone is used only during co-op voice chat, is never recorded, and is never stored.

Sabotage is published by Raveneal Associates. This policy explains what the game collects, why, who it goes to, and how to have it deleted. It applies to the iOS and Android versions of the game.

Who we are

Raveneal Associates. For anything in this policy, including a request to delete your data, write to privacy@ravenealassociates.com.

What the game collects

An account identifier

The first time you open Sabotage it creates an anonymous account in the background. No sign-in screen, nothing to fill in. This is a random identifier that lets the game keep your progress, your salvage and your leaderboard entry on our server rather than in a file on your phone, so the numbers players compete on cannot be edited locally.

Later, and only if you choose to, you can attach Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In to that same account so your progress survives a reinstall or a new phone. We receive the account identifier those services give us. We do not receive, and never ask for, your password. If you use Apple's Hide My Email we never see a real address.

The game has no email-and-password login, so there is nothing to forget and no password of yours for us to hold.

Your handle and your scores

If you choose a handle for the leaderboard, that handle is public, alongside the score and wave you reached and a small set of run statistics — things like your best kill streak, deepest boss, accuracy and salvage earned. That is the whole point of a leaderboard, and none of it is personal unless you make your handle personal. Choose accordingly.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple and Google. We are told that a purchase completed so we can unlock what you bought. We never see your card details, billing address or any payment information.

Advertising

Sabotage shows opt-in rewarded ads only — you press a button to watch one in exchange for something in the game. There are no banners and no interstitials, and nothing is shown unless you ask for it. These are served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising identifier to choose and measure ads. You can limit or reset that identifier in your device settings at any time, and where the law requires it you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are used.

The microphone, in co-op only

Co-op games include voice chat. When it is on, your microphone audio is sent live to the other player through our relay server and is discarded immediately. It is never recorded, never written to disk and never stored. You can turn voice off entirely in Settings — the option is called Open mic in co-op — and co-op remains fully playable in silence.

Basic technical information

Requests to our server carry the ordinary things any web request carries, such as your app version and platform, and an IP address at the moment of the request. We use these to make the game work and to stop abuse.

What the game does not collect

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting or attribution software. There is none in the app.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos, files or calendar.
  • No browsing or app-usage history from outside Sabotage.
  • No recordings of your voice.
  • No email address or password.

How long we keep it

Your account and its progress are kept while the account exists, so that returning to the game returns you to your run. Co-op session traffic is not retained at all. Delete your account and the data attached to it goes with it.

Deleting your account and your data

Write to privacy@ravenealassociates.com from any address, telling us your handle or your account identifier (you can find it in the game's settings). We will delete the account, its progress and its leaderboard entries, and confirm to you when it is done — within 30 days, and normally much sooner.

Deleting your account removes your data. It does not refund purchases; refunds are handled by Apple and Google under their own terms.

Children

Sabotage is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see what data we hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, or to object to how it is used. Ask us at privacy@ravenealassociates.com and we will answer. You will never be charged for asking, and asking will not affect your account.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or who it goes to, we will update this page and change the date at the top. The current version is always the one published here.
