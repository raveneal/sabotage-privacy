Sabotage — Support

Sabotage for iPhone and Android, by Raveneal Associates.

Contact

Email support@ravenealassociates.com. Say which phone you are on and, if it helps, the handle you set in SETTINGS, so we can find your account. We answer within a few days.

Common questions

  • I changed phones and lost my progress. On iPhone, progress lives on your account: Sign in with Apple in SETTINGS on the old phone first, then on the new one, and it comes across. On Android, progress stays with the phone for now.
  • A purchase did not arrive. Relaunch the game with a connection: it re-checks the store's record of what you bought and hands it over. If it still has not arrived, email us with the receipt from Apple or Google.
  • The free-life ad says NO AD AVAILABLE. That is honest: no ad was available at that moment. It is never a lost life; the card offers a life for salvage instead.
  • CO-OP cannot find the room. Both players need a connection, and the guest needs the four-letter room code the host sees, or use QUICK MATCH.
  • How do I delete my account? SETTINGS, then DELETE ACCOUNT, twice. Details on the account deletion page.
